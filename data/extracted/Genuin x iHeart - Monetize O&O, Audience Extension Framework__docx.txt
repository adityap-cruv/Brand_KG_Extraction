This document outlines the commercial and technical framework for the Genuin × iHeart partnership. It covers how iHeart can activate audience extension through Genuin’s platform, the execution models available, and the data governance framework governing the integration.
Partnership Context
iHeart is an established brand partner on the Genuin Network. This document extends that partnership to cover audience extension via Genuin’s publisher inventory, with the Sponsored Takeover as the primary ad format.
Across all models, Genuin manages ad and video content delivery. Targeting decisions are owned by iHeart (direct execution) or by the DSP (programmatic execution). Genuin forwards signals only — no independent targeting is performed by Genuin.

2.1  Sponsored Takeover, Sponsored Post & Programmatic Activation
The Sponsored Takeover is the highest-impact placement on the Genuin platform. It converts the content feed on a Genuin-powered publisher page into a fully branded, session-level experience. For the full duration of a user’s session, all video content served is sourced from iHeart’s creative library on Genuin. Audio ads are served programmatically from Beeswax through Triton TAP.
Creative Specifications
Genuin supports two execution models for the iHeart partnership. The models differ in the publisher relationship structure and who manages publisher inventory. The underlying ad serving and data flow is identical across both.
3.1  Option A — iHeart Network Expansion via Triton(or otherwise)
iHeart uses Genuin-generated Web SDK tags to onboard new publisher domains such as PMC(pmc.com) into the network independently. The same infrastructure powering iHeart’s existing Genuin integration is extended to these new domains.
What Genuin Provides
Web SDK integration tags generated by Genuin for iHeart’s use
Full ad and content serving stack, consistent with iHeart’s existing integration
Runtime stitching of abstract video assets and programmatic audio assets
What iHeart Is Responsible For
Executing a direct commercial agreement with each new publisher domain if it doesn’t exist
Placing the Genuin Web SDK tag on publisher pages and confirming technical deployment
Managing Ads.txt and Sellers.json entries for new domains if they don’t exist
3.2  Option B — Offsite Audience Extension via Genuin Brand Partners
Genuin delivers iHeart’s audience extension across its existing brand network via a session-level sponsored takeover.
What Genuin Provides

Targeting signal collection and forwarding to Triton TAP / Beeswax
Runtime stitching of abstract video assets and programmatic audio assets
What iHeart Is Responsible For
Configuring sponsorship parameters and deal terms in Triton TAP
Setting up audio sponsorship and creative assets in Beeswax

Data Flow Diagram
Genuin collects and forwards contextual, demographic, and device signals at every ad request. Genuin does not apply any targeting logic independently. All targeting decisions are owned by iHeart (direct execution) or by the DSP (programmatic execution).
4.1  Programmatic Execution
In programmatic execution, targeting decisions are owned entirely by Beeswax (the DSP), acting on demand routed through Triton TAP (iHeart’s owned SSP). This model applies to all programmatic sponsored takeover delivery.
Genuin SDK collects signals from Genuin Placement and forwards them to Triton TAP in the ad request
Triton TAP packages these signals into an oRTB 2.x bid request and sends it to Beeswax and other connected DSPs
Beeswax applies its own targeting model — using the bid request signals and its own audience data — and selects the winning creative
Genuin has no role in targeting, audience matching, or bidding in this model
4.2 Non Programmatic Execution
In direct sponsorship execution, iHeart has two options for how targeting decisions are made. Both options rely on signals collected and forwarded by Genuin.
Out of the Box — Contextual & Geo Targeting
By default, Genuin automatically provides the following targeting signals for all direct sponsorships with no additional configuration required:
Geo location (city / DMA / country, derived from IP)
Contextual relevance signals (page category, content keywords, publisher domain)
Device context (type, OS, browser, user agent)
Additional Options — Bring Your Own Algorithm (BYOA)
iHeart can substitute Genuin’s default targeting with its own proprietary algorithm. In BYOA mode, iHeart’s targeting system receives Genuin’s collected signals and makes all decisioning independently.
Genuin forwards geo, demographic, and contextual signals to iHeart’s targeting endpoint
iHeart’s algorithm determines which users to target and at what frequency
Genuin has no targeting role in this model; signal forwarding is the full extent of Genuin’s contribution
BYOA is available for direct sponsorships only — programmatic execution is always DSP-controlled
4.3  Identity Matching
Identity matching enables iHeart to show relevant, personalised content to the right users on Genuin-powered placements. Genuin supplies matching signals with every ad request; iHeart or a third-party identity partner performs the actual resolution.
Matching Signals Available (No PII Exchanged)
Device advertising ID (GAID / IDFA, hashed)
User Agent string
IP address (truncated to /24 for privacy compliance)
Geo location (city / DMA level)
Contextual signals (page category, content keywords)
Probabilistic device fingerprint (non-PII derived)
Option A — Direct Match by iHeart or Triton/Beeswax (Preferred)
Genuin forwards signals server-to-server to iHeart’s(Triton/Beeswax in this case) targeting endpoint. iHeart(Triton/Beeswax) performs the identity match internally using its own user graph. This is the preferred approach as it avoids any third-party data sharing.
Option B — Third-Party Identity Provider (Pre-Mapping)
If iHeart prefers to use a third-party identity graph to establish the user link ahead of runtime, a pre-mapping workflow can be set up. This produces a mapping file (Genuin User ID → iHeart User ID) used at runtime for resolved targeting.
Genuin prepares a pre-mapping dataset with pseudonymous Genuin User IDs and associated non-PII device signals
The dataset is sent to a third-party identity partner via encrypted transfer (TLS-secured API or SFTP)
The identity partner maps Genuin IDs to iHeart’s user IDs (hashed / tokenised) using their cross-ecosystem graph
The resulting mapping file is returned to Genuin and used at runtime for resolved targeting
Genuin operates under a published Privacy Policy and Terms of Service governing all data collection, use, retention, and sharing across both integration options.
Data Collected

User Agent string

1 | Placement enters viewport
A Genuin-powered sponsored takeover becomes visible on the user’s screen on a Genuin-powered iHeart page or partner placement. Genuin Placement initialises and begins collecting contextual and device signals.
2 | Genuin Placement sends signals to Genuin SDK
Collected signals — including IP address, geo, GAID/IDFA, context keywords, device identifiers, and session context — are forwarded to Genuin SDK. No targeting decisions are made; signals are collected and forwarded only.
3 | Genuin SDK sends ad request to Triton TAP
Genuin SDK sends an ad request to Triton TAP, iHeart’s owned SSP, including the collected context signals and placement details.
4 | Triton TAP runs programmatic auction
Triton TAP sends an oRTB 2.x bid request to Beeswax and other connected DSPs. Beeswax applies its targeting model and responds with the winning bid and audio creative. All targeting and decisioning is owned by Beeswax.
5 | VAST tag returned to Genuin SDK
Triton TAP returns the winning VAST tag (audio creative) to Genuin SDK, including impression trackers and the audio creative URL.
6 | Genuin SDK requests video creative from Genuin BCC
Genuin SDK sends a video fetch request to Genuin BCC (Video Post Serving Engine), including placement context, session signals, and geo location.
7 | Genuin BCC returns video creative
Genuin BCC delivers the video creative to Genuin SDK — including the video file URL, playback config, and impression tracking endpoint.
8 | Sponsored takeover renders
Genuin SDK stitches video and audio together client-side and renders the sponsored takeover in the placement. Impressions are fired to Triton TAP and Beeswax. The user’s session is fully covered for its duration.
