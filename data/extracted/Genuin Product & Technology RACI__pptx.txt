--- Slide 2 ---
The single person who signs off on the task, has final decision-making authority, and owns the outcome.
This is usually the project manager or owner. There should only be one person accountable per task.
People who are kept updated on progress, usually after the task is completed or during key milestones.
This is one-way communication, such as stakeholders or management.
People whose opinions are sought, usually subject matter experts (SMEs)
There is semi-regular two-way communication between responsible and consulted teams before the work is completed.
Defining the RACI
The person or team who actually performs the work to complete the task.
There can be multiple people responsible, such as team members executing the work.
