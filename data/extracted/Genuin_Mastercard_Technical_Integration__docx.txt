Overview
Part 1 — What
Mastercard activates brand and merchant advertising across iHeart destinations powered by Genuin’s video infrastructure. Genuin supports multi-channel advertising — encompassing direct-sold sponsored video placements, programmatic in-feed and in-stream video, and LLM-native conversational ads delivered through Genuin’s AI-powered overlay. This document presents the full range of options available to Mastercard to determine the right activation path.
There are two distinct buying mechanisms. For Sponsored Takeover and Sponsored Post, content is served by Genuin’s video infrastructure — Mastercard uploads brand video and Genuin handles delivery. Targeting can be managed by Genuin’s out-of-the-box system or delegated to Mastercard’s own algorithm (BYOA). For programmatic in-feed and in-stream placements, Mastercard’s DSP serves ad creatives directly in real time via Aniview — the DSP controls both creative selection and audience targeting.
Every impression is eligible for identity mapping to Mastercard’s user graph via LiveRamp, enabling audience-level targeting and closed-loop measurement.

2. In-Feed Ad Placements
In-feed ads appear as discrete cards within the Genuin video carousel — interleaved with organic content at configured slot positions, or taking over the first N slots. The five available formats span the two buying mechanisms: direct-sold formats served by Genuin, and programmatic formats served by Mastercard’s DSP via Aniview.
2.1  Format Overview
2.2  Sponsored Takeover
The Sponsored Takeover is the highest-impact in-feed placement. It converts the first N slots of the video feed into a continuous Mastercard-sponsored experience, with all N positions served by Genuin’s video infrastructure. Targeting is managed by Genuin’s out-of-the-box system or, for advanced per-user creative personalisation, by Mastercard’s own algorithm via BYOA (see Section 5.3).
2.3  Sponsored Post
Sponsored Posts are individual in-feed cards containing Mastercard-branded video content, served by Genuin’s video infrastructure at calculated ad-slot positions, interleaved with organic content. Targeting options (Genuin out-of-the-box or BYOA) mirror those available for Sponsored Takeover.
2.4  Programmatic In-Feed Video
Programmatic in-feed video ads are served by Mastercard’s DSP via Google’s IMA SDK and fulfilled by the Aniview ad server. They appear as video cards with a CTA bar replacing engagement icons. The DSP controls audience targeting and creative selection in real time.
2.5  Programmatic In-Feed Native
Static card-format creatives served via GPT.js and Aniview. Native card layout — headline, image, brand name, description, and CTA — blends with the feed aesthetic. Targeting and creative selection handled by the DSP.
2.6  Programmatic In-Feed Display
Standard banner creatives (image or HTML5) as feed cards, served via GPT.js and Aniview. Creative selection and targeting handled by the DSP.
2.7  Aniview Integration Paths (Programmatic)
Programmatic in-feed formats (Sections 2.4–2.6) are fulfilled via Aniview. Three integration paths are available. PMP / Programmatic Guaranteed is the recommended starting point.
3. In-Stream Ad Placements
In-stream ads are linear video ads that play within the Genuin video carousel player — before, during, or after organic content videos. They are a Mechanism 2 (programmatic) placement: Mastercard’s DSP serves creatives in real time via Aniview, controlling both audience targeting and creative selection.

4.1  How the LLM Overlay Works
Genuin embeds an AI-powered conversational interface (Octo GPT) on top of the video feed on iHeart destinations. The overlay is triggered by a configurable dwell period — after a user has been engaged with the feed for a set duration, an AI-generated prompt appears.
The prompt is auto-generated from page context, the video being viewed, and user interaction history. When the prompt fires, the conversation box expands to show the AI response, a Mastercard ad unit, and additional video content. Users can dismiss the overlay at any time and continue the conversation freely.

6. Measurement & Attribution
Genuin provides a baseline set of performance metrics out of the box for all ad formats. Two additional attribution layers are available: Context Attribution, which measures the influence of pre-exposure content on sponsored content performance, and view attribution via the Genuin Pixel solution.
Part 1 — Genuin Out-of-the-Box Metrics
Available by default for all placements, accessible via the Genuin BCC dashboard and API export.
Part 2 — Context Attribution
Context Attribution tracks the video content a user consumed immediately before viewing a sponsored placement. It captures the relationship between the pre-exposure content journey and conversion behaviour — enabling Mastercard to understand which content contexts are most conducive to sponsored content conversion.
Context Attribution data is surfaced in BCC as a contextual performance report. Mastercard can use these insights to prioritise placement after content types that consistently drive higher conversion rates for a given offer or merchant.
Part 3 — View Attribution via Genuin Pixel
Genuin Pixel is a lightweight JavaScript tag placed on Mastercard’s merchant pages. It links Genuin ad impressions to downstream user actions on Mastercard’s properties, without requiring a click.
6.1  Pixel Implementation
Tag Placement: Genuin provides the Pixel as a standard JavaScript snippet. Place it on all Mastercard merchant and conversion destination pages.
Event Configuration: Configure conversion event triggers in BCC — each event maps to a specific user action (e.g., a “Thank You” page load, a button click, or a form submission).
Go Live: Once live, the Pixel attributes Genuin ad impressions to conversion events within the configured view-through attribution window.
Reporting: Attributed conversion data is available in BCC reports and via API export. Events can optionally be streamed to Mastercard’s data infrastructure.

7 | Content & Ads Serve — Sponsored Takeover and Sponsored Post: Genuin’s video infrastructure serves the brand content. Programmatic in-feed and in-stream: Mastercard’s DSP serves ad creatives via Aniview in real time.

Path A
VAST / Ad Tags | Mastercard provides a VAST tag or ad tag URL configured directly as a demand source in Aniview. At ad break time, Aniview requests the tag and serves the returned creative.
Best for: Simplest activation. Ideal when Mastercard has existing ad tags from their own ad server.
Path B
PMP / Programmatic Guaranteed
★ Recommended | Mastercard creates Private Marketplace (PMP) or Programmatic Guaranteed (PG) deals in their DSP. The resulting deal IDs are accepted on Aniview. Impressions transact at a negotiated CPM against a guaranteed or prioritised allocation of iHeart inventory.
Best for: Preferred for guaranteed delivery and fixed pricing. Maintains Mastercard’s DSP workflow while securing premium inventory.
Path C
DSP oRTB Bidding | Mastercard’s DSP connects to Aniview via an oRTB (OpenRTB) endpoint. The DSP receives real-time bid requests for iHeart inventory and responds with bids. Winning bids are served by Aniview.
Best for: Maximum flexibility and real-time optimisation. Suitable for programmatic-first teams.
Position | Behaviour
Pre-roll | Ad plays before content video begins. Ad request sent at content start.
Mid-roll | Ad interrupts content at the midpoint. Ad request prefetched ~10–12 seconds before the break.
Post-roll | Ad plays after content video completes. Ad request prefetched ~10 seconds before content ends.
Genuin uses a non-blocking prefetch model — ads are requested in advance of the break point, so content continues playing while the ad loads. This minimises latency and avoids buffering gaps.

Expandable | A compact teaser unit that expands to reveal full ad content when the user taps or interacts. Allows richer creative without disrupting the conversation flow.

Option A
Genuin Out-of-the-Box | Genuin’s targeting system uses the audience segments resolved via LiveRamp to match users to the appropriate Sponsored placement. When a user is identified as belonging to a configured Mastercard segment, Genuin serves the relevant Sponsored Takeover or Post content from the uploaded video library. Mastercard configures the audience and creative mapping in BCC; Genuin handles delivery and optimisation automatically.
Option B
Bring Your Own Algorithm (BYOA) | Mastercard’s own targeting algorithm decides which video to serve for each user in each context. At the moment a Sponsored slot is ready to fill, Genuin sends a real-time context payload to a server-side endpoint provided by Mastercard. Mastercard’s server responds with the video ID(s) to serve. This gives Mastercard full control over creative personalisation — enabling use cases such as merchant-specific video selection, spend-category-matched content, or geo-specific creative.
