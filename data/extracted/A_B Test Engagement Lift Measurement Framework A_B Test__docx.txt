Engagement Lift Measurement Framework A/B Test
Use case: Measure the impact of in-stream ads on engagement for MCC videos distributed through the Genuin carousel.
1. Objective
Determine whether showing in-stream ads in Genuin-hosted MCC videos:
increases monetization value, and
maintains engagement within an acceptable threshold compared with a no-ads experience.
2. Test Design
Variants
Control (No Ads)
MCC uploads videos to Genuin
MCC places Genuin carousel on site
Genuin-hosted videos appear in carousel
On play, no VAST request is sent
Content starts immediately
Treatment (Ads)
Same setup as control
On play, player sends VAST request to ad server
In-stream ad is returned when eligible/fill is available
Ad is shown before or during content per defined ad logic
Traffic Split
50% of eligible traffic → Ads
50% of eligible traffic → No Ads
Randomization
Use user-level or session-level randomization, not impression-level.
Reason: the same user should not bounce between ad and no-ad experiences within the same visit.
Consistency Requirements
Keep these identical across both groups:
carousel placement
UI and design
autoplay behavior
recommendation logic
content pool
page templates
device eligibility rules
player size and player behavior
3. Experiment Hypothesis
Primary hypothesis
The Ads variant will increase revenue per session while keeping engagement degradation within an acceptable tolerance relative to No Ads.
Example business threshold
Revenue/session increases by at least 15%
Average watch time/session decreases by no more than 3%
Completion rate decreases by no more than 2%
You can tune these thresholds based on MCC goals.
4. Primary Success Metrics
Pick one engagement metric and one monetization metric as primary.
Primary Engagement KPI
Average Watch Time per Eligible Session
Formula:
total content watch time / eligible sessions
Why this is strong:
reflects actual consumption
not overly inflated by accidental clicks
captures ad friction impact indirectly
Primary Monetization KPI
Revenue per Eligible Session
Formula:
total ad revenue / eligible sessions
Why this matters:
directly ties experiment to commercial outcome
easier to compare against engagement tradeoff
5. Secondary Metrics
Funnel Metrics
Carousel Impression Count
Carousel CTR = carousel clicks / carousel impressions
Video Start Rate = video starts / carousel impressions
Ad Request Rate = ad requests / video starts
Ad Fill Rate = filled ad responses / ad requests
Ad Impression Rate = ad impressions / video starts
Content Start Rate = content starts / video starts
Completion Rate = completed views / content starts
Videos per Session
Average Session Dwell Time
Exit Rate after Play Start
Return Visitor Rate if identity/session stitching exists
Quality / Experience Guardrails
player error rate
ad error rate
ad timeout rate
buffering rate
page abandonment after ad start
mute/unmute behavior if relevant
first content frame time
6. Key Business Readouts
At decision time, compare:
Engagement change
Revenue change
Net tradeoff
Lift Formula
For any KPI:
Lift % = ((Treatment / Control) - 1) × 100
Examples:
Watch time lift
Completion rate lift/drop
Revenue per session lift
7. Recommended Decision Rule
A clean decision model:
Launch Ads broadly if:
Revenue/session is significantly higher
Watch time/session is within acceptable decline threshold
Completion rate and start rate do not materially collapse
Technical quality remains healthy
Do not launch / iterate if:
Revenue lift is weak
Engagement loss is larger than acceptable
Fill or timeout behavior damages UX
Revenue gain does not offset engagement cost

9. Core Events
Carousel Events
1. carousel_impression
Triggered when carousel is viewable or rendered based on agreed measurement rule.
Properties:
carousel_id
placement_id
page_type
position_on_page
viewability_status
2. carousel_item_click
Triggered when a user clicks/taps a video in the carousel.
Properties:
video_id
item_position
content_category
thumbnail_type
Player / Content Events
3. video_player_load
Triggered when player initializes.
Properties:
video_id
player_id
autoplay
muted
player_size
4. video_start
Triggered when playback starts from user intent.
Properties:
video_id
start_type (click, autoplay, etc.)
5. content_start
Triggered when actual editorial content begins.
This is important because in ad-supported flows, video_start and content_start are not the same thing.
6. content_progress
Triggered at milestones:
25%
50%
75%
Properties:
progress_pct
watch_time_seconds
7. content_complete
Triggered when content reaches completion rule.
Properties:
watch_time_seconds
completion_pct
8. video_exit
Triggered when user exits player or page before completion.
Properties:
exit_reason (close, scroll_away, navigate_away, error, ad_abandonment)
watch_time_seconds
Ad Events
9. ad_request
Triggered when VAST call is sent.
Properties:
ad_server
ad_unit_id
request_id
ad_position (pre-roll, mid-roll)
timeout_setting_ms
10. ad_response
Triggered when ad server responds.
Properties:
fill_status (filled, empty, error)
creative_id
advertiser
campaign_id

11. ad_impression
Triggered when ad impression is validly served.
Properties:
creative_id
campaign_id
ad_duration_seconds
skippable
12. ad_start
Triggered when ad playback begins.
13. ad_complete
Triggered when ad finishes.
Properties:
ad_watch_time_seconds
14. ad_skip
Triggered when user skips.
Properties:
skip_offset_seconds
15. ad_error
Triggered on ad playback/render failure.
Properties:
error_code
error_message
Revenue Events
16. ad_revenue
Triggered when revenue is attributed.
Properties:
revenue_amount
currency

campaign_id

13. Experiment Guardrails
Exclude:
bots / invalid traffic
internal QA traffic
broken placements
sessions with missing experiment assignment
sessions where variant flips mid-session
severe player errors that invalidate comparison
Also ensure:
experiment assignment is sticky
content mix is balanced across variants
ad frequency logic is controlled
both groups run in parallel over the same dates

15. Sample Interpretation
Example outcome:
Revenue/session: +22%
Avg watch time/session: -1.8%
Completion rate: -0.9%
Videos/session: -0.5%
Interpretation:
monetization materially improved
engagement degradation stayed within threshold
rollout is likely justified
Another example:
Revenue/session: +8%
Avg watch time/session: -7%
Completion rate: -5%
Interpretation:
monetization gain probably does not justify UX cost
test suggests ad load, latency, or ad placement needs adjustment
16. Practical Implementation Notes for Genuin + MCC
Given your flow, the most important distinction is:
player started
vs
content actually started
Because the ad-supported flow adds a pre-content step, measuring only starts can hide engagement loss.
So your reporting should always separate:
play intent
ad exposure
content consumption
That is the heart of proving true engagement lift or drop.
17. Suggested One-Line Test Statement
“Measure whether enabling in-stream ads for Genuin-hosted MCC videos increases revenue per session while preserving watch time and completion within agreed engagement thresholds versus a no-ads control.”
