OCTO CANVAS  ·  VISION & POSITIONING
Vibe Design for Generative Destinations
A vibe design platform for generative brand destinations. Octo Canvas guides marketers through a two-phase process: a Design phase — iterative dialogue producing sitemap, wireframe, and visual design on a live canvas — followed by a Build phase that auto-deploys Genuin SDK implementation. The output is a living destination that draws from brand capabilities at runtime, maintained entirely by Genuin post-launch.

OCTO CANVAS  ·  ARCHITECTURE
Two Layers, One Experience
Genuin's Intelligence & Infrastructure Layer
Agent Orchestration
Routes generative destination intent to Octo Canvas. Manages all agent-to-agent coordination across Octo products.
Growth Intelligence
Personalisation engine, community infrastructure, workflow orchestration. Knows how to grow and engage a brand.
UX Capabilities
SDK components configurable via JSON — video, chat, text, images. Every visual element the destination renders.
Shared Infrastructure
Common agent foundation for all Octo products. OctoGPT and Octo Canvas are configurations on this shared layer.
Brand's Identity & Knowledge Layer on Genuin
Brand Knowledge
Source of identity, persona, and tonality for the destination.
BCC Knowledge, communities, social accounts, uploaded files.
Brand Capabilities
CMS connections, podcast feeds, contest APIs, event data. Isolated per brand — never co-mingled with other brands.
Persistent Brand Identity
Stored once in BCC, shared across all Octo products. OctoGPT and Octo Canvas reference the same profile.
Capability Pathways
Generate capabilities from brand knowledge, or connect from the 9,900+ Capabilities Marketplace.
At design time, Octo reasons about what the destination needs & makes those decisions brand-specific. At runtime, personalisation & live data comes together to grow and engage the audience.

--- Slide 4 ---
OCTO CANVAS  ·  INFRASTRUCTURE & WORKFLOW
How Octo Canvas Works
INFRASTRUCTURE
①
Genuin Buildtime Infrastructure
Genuin Octo Canvas, powered by the brand's Octo Capabilities and Brand Knowledge. Capabilities are recommended and auto-generated to design a vibe that resonates with the brand
②
Genuin Runtime
Infrastructure
Genuin’s Video & Community Infrastructure implemented using SDKs. Direct integrations into client CMS systems — destination content is live, fresh, and personalised at runtime, never a static snapshot.
WORKFLOW
01
Define the Objective
Brand describes goal in natural language. Agent confirms destination purpose.
02
Establish Brand Identity
Agent surfaces Brand Knowledge from BCC — connected sources, content, and generated capabilities.
03
Define Persona & Tonality
Persona and tone emerge from Brand Knowledge. Agent presents and refines conversationally.
05
Create the Sitemap
Agent proposes full page architecture. Brand reviews and refines through conversation.
06
Create the Wireframe
Agent proposes structural layout per page. Brand shapes structure and content placement.
08
Generate Implementation
Approved design translated into full Genuin SDK implementation. Code view available for review.
09
QA Review → Deploy
Live on QA subdomain for approval. One confirmation deploys to production on Genuin infrastructure.
