1. Background
In 2019, iHeartRadio partnered with Concourse Media to launch iHeartVIP.com, a destination designed to distribute music and industry news using a content delivery format similar to editorial publishing platforms.
The initiative saw positive engagement metrics, but iHeartMedia eventually decided not to continue the publishing initiative, and the domain was deprioritized.
Today, with the evolution of short-form video, creator engagement, and community-driven experiences, there is an opportunity to revive iHeartVIP as a modern fan engagement hub powered by Genuin.
2. Vision
The goal is to revive the iHeartVIP.com domain, transforming it from its 2019 music/industry news roots into a high-engagement video and community platform. By leveraging Genuin’s infrastructure, we will enable 1,000+ event-based communities, integrated contests, and streamlined monetization. This revival will be achieved by white-labeling a Genuin-powered subdomain with the iheartvip.com domain.
3. Strategic Opportunity
iHeart runs 1000+ live events annually, including:
Music festivals
Artist tours
Radio station activations
Brand partner events
Podcast tours
Sponsored experiences
Currently these activations are distributed across fragmented channels. Reviving iHeartVIP allows iHeart to create a centralized fan engagement platform where events, content, and communities converge.

5. What Does “Render Genuin Platform at iHeartVIP” Mean?
The core idea is to white-label the revived iHeartVIP domain using our existing Genuin web platform infrastructure. Once the domain mapping is configured, the iHeartVIP domain will point to our web application (currently hosted under the iHeart brand on Genuin). This will allow the iHeartVIP experience to be delivered directly through the iheartvip.com domain, while being powered by our platform in the backend. As a result, iHeart can quickly activate communities, video experiences, and monetization opportunities on iHeartVIP without needing to build a new platform from scratch.

6. Approach 1
Enable Subdomain on Existing iHeart Brand (BCC)
What
Reuse the existing iHeart production brand inside BCC and update its subdomain.
Current:
iheartmedia.begenuin.com
Proposed:
iheartvip.begenuin.com
This would allow iHeartVIP to run on the same brand instance currently powering iHeart.
Why
• Avoid managing multiple brands in BCC
 • Reduce content duplication
 • Reuse existing content pipelines
 • Faster operational setup
This approach keeps everything under the same brand infrastructure.
How (Technical Steps)
Engineering tasks required:
Risks
Share URL Changes
Existing URLs: iheart.com/podcast/...
Potential new URLs: iheartvip.com/podcast/...
Mitigation:
• Implement URL redirects
 • Support both URL structures
Loss of Existing Subdomain
Current subdomain:iheartmedia.begenuin.com
If replaced, iHeart cannot white-label it separately later.
Although iHeart previously indicated they do not need this, it still introduces future limitation risk.
7. Approach 2
Create Separate Brand for iHeartVIP
What
Create a new brand inside BCC dedicated to iHeartVIP.
Example:
Brand 1 → iHeartMedia (existing)
Brand 2 → iHeartVIP
New subdomain:
iheartvip.begenuin.com
This can then be white-labeled under iheartvip.com.
Why
This approach provides clean separation and avoids modifying production infrastructure.
Benefits:
• Minimal risk to existing iHeart production brand
 • No changes required to existing share URLs
 • Simpler implementation for engineering
 • Cleaner architecture for future scaling
Risks
Operational Overhead
iHeart/Genuin may need to manage two brands inside BCC.
