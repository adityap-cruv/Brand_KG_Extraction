Overview
Part 1 — What
Mastercard activates brand and merchant advertising across iHeart destinations powered by Genuin’s video infrastructure. Genuin supports multi-channel advertising — encompassing direct-sold sponsored video placements, programmatic in-feed and in-stream video, and LLM-native conversational ads delivered through Genuin’s AI-powered overlay. This document presents the full range of options available to Mastercard to determine the right activation path.
Genuin serves selected video posts to targeted users on iHeart’s destination — drawing from the content available within Genuin’s video infrastructure and delivering it to the right audiences. For direct-sold placements (Sponsored Takeover and Sponsored Post), Mastercard’s brand video is uploaded and served by Genuin’s video infrastructure. For programmatic in-feed and in-stream placements, Mastercard’s DSP serves the required ad creatives in real time via Aniview — Genuin provides the inventory and the ad slot, while the DSP controls creative selection and bidding.
Every ad impression is eligible for mapping to Mastercard’s identity graph via LiveRamp, enabling audience-level targeting and closed-loop measurement.

2. In-Feed Ad Placements
In-feed ads appear as discrete cards within the Genuin video carousel — interleaved with organic content at configured slot positions, or taking over the first N slots of the feed. Five in-feed ad formats are available to Mastercard, spanning direct-sold sponsored content served by Genuin and fully programmatic placements served by Mastercard’s DSP via Aniview.
2.1  Format Overview
2.2  Sponsored Takeover
The Sponsored Takeover is the highest-impact in-feed placement on the Genuin platform. It converts the first N slots of the video feed into a continuous Mastercard-sponsored experience — all N positions show Mastercard’s video content served by Genuin’s video infrastructure. The Takeover overrides all other in-feed ad types for its occupied slots and is a direct-sold placement.
2.3  Sponsored Post
Sponsored Posts are individual in-feed cards containing Mastercard-branded video content served by Genuin’s video infrastructure. Unlike the Takeover, Sponsored Posts follow standard slot frequency logic — appearing at calculated ad-slot positions within the feed, interleaved with organic content. They are the primary direct-sold in-feed format for ongoing placements.
2.4  Programmatic In-Feed Video
Programmatic in-feed video ads are video creatives served by Mastercard’s DSP via Google’s IMA SDK and fulfilled by the Aniview ad server. They appear as video cards within the feed with a CTA bar replacing the standard engagement icons. The DSP controls audience targeting and creative selection in real time.
2.5  Programmatic In-Feed Native
Programmatic in-feed native ads are static card-format creatives served via GPT.js and fulfilled by Aniview. They use a native card layout — headline, image, brand name, description, and CTA — that blends with the feed’s card aesthetic. Targeting and creative selection handled by the DSP.
2.6  Programmatic In-Feed Display
Programmatic in-feed display ads serve standard banner creatives (image or HTML5) as feed cards using GPT.js and Aniview. The display banner occupies the full card slot, rendered as a static visual unit. Creative selection and targeting handled by the DSP.
2.7  Aniview Integration Paths
Programmatic in-feed ad formats (Sections 2.4–2.6) are fulfilled via the Aniview ad server. Mastercard can connect to Aniview through any of the following three integration paths — the choice depends on Mastercard’s existing ad operations infrastructure and commercial preference. PMP / Programmatic Guaranteed is the recommended starting point.
3. In-Stream Ad Placements
In-stream ads are linear video ads that play within the Genuin video carousel player — before, during, or after organic content videos. They run programmatically through the Aniview ad server via VAST or VMAP, with Mastercard’s DSP controlling creative selection and bidding.

4. LLM Ads (via Koah)
4.1  How the LLM Overlay Works
Genuin embeds an AI-powered conversational interface (OctoGPT) on top of the video feed on iHeart destinations. The overlay is triggered by a configurable dwell period — after a user has been engaged with the feed for a set duration, an AI-generated prompt appears.
The prompt is auto-generated from page context, the video being viewed, and user interaction history — making it contextually relevant to what the user is consuming. When the prompt fires, the conversation box expands to show the AI response, a branded Mastercard ad unit, and additional video content — all within the overlay interface. Users can dismiss the overlay at any time and can continue the conversation with the AI freely.

Part 2 — Extended Attribution via Genuin Pixel
Genuin Pixel is a lightweight JavaScript tag placed on Mastercard’s merchant pages and conversion destinations. It enables post-exposure attribution — linking Genuin ad impressions and clicks to downstream user actions on Mastercard’s properties.
6.1  Pixel Implementation
Tag Placement: Genuin provides the Pixel tag as a standard JavaScript snippet. The snippet is placed on all Mastercard merchant and conversion destination pages.
Event Configuration: Conversion event triggers are configured in Genuin infrastructure — each event maps to a specific user action (e.g., a “Thank You” page load, a button click, a form submission).
Go Live: Once live, the Pixel begins attributing Genuin ad exposures to conversion events within the configured attribution windows.
Part 3 — Context Attribution
Context Attribution tracks the video content a user consumed immediately before viewing a sponsored placement. It captures the relationship between the pre-exposure content journey and conversion behaviour — enabling Mastercard to understand which content contexts are most conducive to sponsored content conversion.

Best for: Preferred for guaranteed delivery and fixed pricing. Maintains Mastercard’s DSP workflow while securing premium inventory.

Best for: Maximum flexibility and real-time optimisation. Suitable for programmatic-first teams that want dynamic bidding on iHeart inventory.

LLM ads are a distinctive, high-attention ad format unique to Genuin’s platform — native conversational placements served within an AI-powered chat overlay on top of the video player, activated through Genuin’s partnership with Koah.
1 | Dwell Trigger — After a configurable dwell period on the Genuin feed, the Octo GPT overlay is triggered.
2 | Auto-Prompt Generated — Koah generates a contextual prompt using page, video, and user context signals.
3 | Prompt Box Expands — The overlay expands — the user sees the AI response, a Mastercard ad unit, and video recommendations within the conversation interface.
4 | Ad Served — Koah selects and serves the appropriate Mastercard ad format within the conversation, contextualised to the user’s current interaction in real time.
5 | User Engages or Dismisses — The user can interact with the ad, continue the conversation, or dismiss the overlay to return to the feed.
Format | Description
Action Card | A card-format ad unit with one or more action buttons. Mastercard configures the CTA label (e.g., “Apply Now”, “Get Offer”, “Learn More”) and destination URL. Designed to drive direct user action from within the conversation.
Image Card | A visually-led card featuring a brand image, headline, and optional description. Best suited for brand awareness and visual storytelling within the conversational context.
Poll | An interactive poll embedded within the LLM conversation. Users select from pre-defined options configured by Mastercard. Results can inform audience insight and engagement measurement.
Expandable | A compact teaser unit that expands to reveal full ad content when the user taps or interacts. Allows richer creative without disrupting the conversation flow — the full ad is only shown on user intent.
