Transactional Transparency: No Added Tech Tax
3 Ways to activate media offsite in-network: Transparent pass-throughs to network partners
1
Pay Genuin Direct
Marketer pays Genuin
Marketer pays Genuin directly for all campaigns. Genuin generates the invoice and resolves payouts to all network publishers.
Full visibility into where every dollar goes…  iHeart, Grubhub, Us Weekly, and every partner in the network.
↓  Publishers (pass-through)
or
2
Pay Publisher Direct
Marketer pays the Publisher
Marketer leverages their existing direct relationship and payment setup with the publisher.
Cleanest path for teams with established partnerships. No new billing relationship required with Genuin.
↓  Publishers (direct)
or
3
Pay via RMN
Marketer pays their RMN
Marketer pays their Retail Media Network directly. The RMN pays Genuin via a Genuin-generated invoice.
Advertiser is responsible for ensuring payment resolution. Genuin resolves all publisher payouts.
↓  RMN → Genuin → Publishers
HOW GENUIN EARNS REVENUE
In ALL cases, the Publisher pays
Genuin for Monetized Infrastructure.
Advertisers pay ZERO infrastructure fees. Forever.
All advertiser media payments are a direct, transparent pass-through to our network partners (iHeart, Grubhub, Us Weekly, and 300+ more). Genuin's revenue comes exclusively from publisher infrastructure agreements.
