What Are the Different User Experiences in Genuin?
Overview
Genuin is built for teams that want to launch, operate, and scale a brand-owned Community Media Network, not just publish content. Because a Community Media Network spans strategy, content, technology, governance, and monetization, no single team owns it end to end.
Use this guide to:
Understand which Genuin experience applies to your role
Know where to start inside the platform
See how different teams work together across the Community Media Network lifecycle
This matters because Genuin delivers its full value only when these experiences operate as a connected, closed loop, rather than as isolated tools or workflows.
One Platform, Purpose-Built Experiences
Genuin is a unified platform that powers Community Media Networks, retail media experiences, and commerce-driven video engagement through a modular, full‑stack architecture.
Depending on your role, you may interact with:
Brand Control Center for operating and governing the network
Content ingestion, curation, and moderation workflows
SDKs and APIs for embedding and personalization
Onsite and offsite community feed experiences
Analytics, reporting, and monetization systems
Each experience is purpose-built to solve a specific problem, but all connect back to the same underlying system of intelligence, governance, and data.
Primary User Experiences in Genuin
1. Brand & Business Experience (Publishers and Brands)
Owning the network, not just publishing content
Who this is for
Publishers operating brand-owned Community Media Networks
Brands and enterprises building owned communities
Growth, marketing, revenue, and operations teams
The problem this experience solves
Publishers and brands want to own distribution, audience relationships, and monetization. Traditional social platforms fragment workflows, restrict control, and lock away data.
This experience exists to help teams operate their own Community Media Network end to end, from structure and governance to growth and revenue.
Primary objectives
Build and scale brand-owned communities and groups
Aggregate content from creators, partners, and owned libraries
Drive engagement, loyalty, and conversion
Maintain brand safety, governance, and editorial standards
Monetize engagement without losing control or data ownership
Where this happens
Brand Control Center - the operating system for network setup, management, and performance
What teams do in Brand Control Center
Create and manage communities and groups
Configure structure, access, and permissions
Curate content using AI with human-in-the-loop approval
Moderate content and users at scale
Activate sponsorships, commerce, and advertising
Track engagement, growth, and monetization performance
2. Retail & Commerce Media Network Experience
Operating a media network inside commerce environments
Who this is for
Retailers and commerce platforms
Media, monetization, and partnerships teams
The problem this experience solves
Retailers are no longer just sellers of products, they are operators of media networks that host multiple brands and advertisers.
This experience focuses on running a commerce-driven Community Media Network, not managing a single brand presence.
Primary objectives
Increase time-on-site and session depth
Influence purchase decisions through trusted content
Convert engagement into measurable commerce outcomes
Unlock brand-safe, first-party media revenue
How this experience shows up
Community-powered video feeds embedded across:
Product Listing Pages (PLPs)
Product Detail Pages (PDPs)
Category and discovery pages
Contextual feeds aligned to product, category, and shopper intent
Consent-based monetization inside owned properties
Key outcomes
Higher content discoverability
Conversion lift driven by community trust
Improved Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
Reduced Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Key distinction:
Publishers and brands operate their own presence.
Retail and commerce teams operate a network that hosts multiple brands and advertisers.
3. Product, Engineering & Developer Experience
Embedding Genuin without compromising performance or control
Who this is for
Product managers
Frontend and backend engineers
Platform and integration teams
The problem this experience solves
Teams need to embed community and video experiences into existing web and app environments while preserving performance, flexibility, and architectural control.
Primary objectives
Integrate Genuin seamlessly across platforms
Enable context-aware personalization
Maintain scalability and reliability
Where this happens
SDK documentation (Web, iOS, Android, React, Flutter)
API documentation
Integration and implementation guides
Key capabilities
White-labeled, configurable embeds
Placement, feed, and player configuration
API-driven personalization and targeting
Data pipelines to analytics and storage systems

4. Creator & Partner Experience
Contributing content inside trusted brand ecosystems
Who this is for
Creators and influencers
Brand and media partners
The problem this experience solves
Creators want reach and credibility without losing attribution or being buried by opaque algorithms.
Primary objectives
Publish and distribute content
Participate in brand-owned communities
Reach relevant audiences in a brand-safe environment
How creators engage
Social account syncing and uploads
Clip It for short-form video creation
Participation in curated communities and campaigns
Clear attribution and visibility within publisher ecosystems
5. Moderator & Community Manager Experience
Scaling trust, relevance, and governance
Who this is for
Moderation teams
Trust and safety teams
Community managers
The problem this experience solves
As Community Media Networks scale, manual moderation alone does not. This experience balances AI efficiency with human oversight.
Primary objectives
Maintain content quality and compliance
Enforce platform, brand, and community guidelines
Keep communities relevant and trusted
Where this happens
Curate > Activity and Task Timeline views
Key capabilities
Review content by lifecycle state
Approve, reject, or move content
Apply rejection reasons and governance rules
Leverage AI-assisted classification and moderation
6. End-User Experience (Consumers)
Engaging without leaving the brand ecosystem
Who this is for
Consumers visiting publisher or brand websites and apps
The problem this experience solves
Consumers want authentic, relevant content without intrusive ads or forced redirection to external platforms.
What consumers experience
Scrollable, immersive video feeds
Community- and group-based discovery
Shoppable and actionable calls to action
A trust-first, non-disruptive content experience
How These Experiences Work Together
Genuin is designed as a closed-loop system, not disconnected tools:
Brand builds > Creator contributes > AI curates > Product embeds > Consumer engages > Data feeds intelligence
This loop enables publishers and brands to:
Own the audience relationship
Learn continuously from behavior and performance
Optimize content, communities, and monetization over time
This is how Genuin turns engagement into scalable, measurable business outcomes.
