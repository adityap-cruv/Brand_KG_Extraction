"type": Ad insertion type (midroll, preroll, postroll)
"delivery-method": Ad delivery method (download, streaming) | "episode-url": Full URL of the podcast episode being played (e.g., “https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1170-the-joe-show-63164373/episode/ashleys-fights-with-listener-303886757”)
"episode-title": Episode identifier / title ID (e.g., 303886757)
"series-title": Podcast or series identifier (e.g., 63164373) | "ua": User-agent string of the requesting client [UA]

Step4, Ad Response Payload: Triton → App(Genuin SDK(IMA SDK))
IMA SDK(integrated by Genuin SDK) receives audio ad(from Triton servers)
VAST Metadata | Linear Creative | Companion Resources
"VAST version": IAB VAST standard version (4.3)
"ad.id": Unique identifier for the ad unit
"adtitle": Human-readable title of the ad creative
"adsystem": Name of the ad serving system (ODSPY)
"advertiser": Advertiser domain name (odspy.com)
"category": IAB content categories (IAB11-4, IAB1-1)
"expires": Ad validity window in seconds (1000)
"impression": 4 impression tracking URLs fired on ad load | "duration": Total audio ad length (00:00:29.698)
"mediafile.type": Audio MIME type (audio/mpeg)
"mediafile.delivery": Delivery method (progressive)
"mediafile.bitrate": Audio bitrate in kbps (128)
"error": Error tracking URL with %5BERRORCODE%5D macro | "companion sizes": 160x600, 180x150, 300x50, 300x250, 300x600, 320x50, 728x90, 970x90, 970x100, 970x250
"staticresource": PNG banner per companion size
"htmlresource": Animated GIF banner with impression pixels
"iframeresource": iFrame delivery option per size
"trackingevents": creativeView fired on companion impression (4 URLs per size)
"companionclickthrough": Click-through tracking URL per companion size

"cb": Cache-buster timestamp appended to all URLs
