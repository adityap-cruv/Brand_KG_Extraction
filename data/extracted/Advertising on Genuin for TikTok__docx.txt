Overview
Genuin powers short-form video infrastructure for a network of premium brands. Each brand integrates Genuin’s SDK, embedding a video feed natively within their app or website. This document outlines the advertising opportunities available to TikTok across that network — the formats on offer and how they work.
1. SDK Integration & Placement Rendering
Genuin’s SDK is embedded in each in-network publisher’s app or web property. It renders the Genuin-powered video feed. Brands integrate the SDK once; all placement and targeting logic is managed server-side by Genuin.

2. Sponsored Takeover
The Sponsored Takeover is Genuin’s highest-impact placement. TikTok’s brand videos take over the entire content feed on a selected in-network brand — every video slot a user encounters is a TikTok-served experience. There is no competing content in the feed during a Takeover, giving TikTok 100% share of voice for the duration of the session.

3. Sponsored Post
Sponsored Posts are TikTok-branded video cards that appear naturally within the organic content feed, interleaved with editorial content at calculated positions. Unlike the Takeover, organic content continues to appear around the ad — the Sponsored Post is designed to look and feel native to the feed experience, creating brand moments that blend into the user’s natural scroll.

4. Programmatic Audience Extension
Programmatic Audience Extension allows TikTok to extend its existing programmatic campaigns to Genuin’s programmatically enabled in-network brands. TikTok’s demand is integrated directly into each brand’s unified ad auction at the ad server level — and given top priority in the programmatic stack. This means TikTok’s ads are first in line for every eligible impression, ahead of all other programmatic demand sources, across both in-feed video and in-stream video placements.
4.1  In-Feed Video
TikTok’s video creatives appear as native cards within the Genuin-powered content feed, rendered by the IMA SDK. The ad is delivered in response to the highest-priority bid in the unified auction and plays automatically as the user scrolls to it.
Creative Specifications
4.2  In-Stream Video
TikTok’s creatives run as linear video ads around content on in-network brand properties — before the content begins (pre-roll), at natural breaks within it (mid-roll), or after it concludes (post-roll). In-stream inventory is available at the top of the unified auction, giving TikTok first-look priority across all three positions.

5. Data Privacy & Targeting Controls
A core principle of the Genuin partnership model is that TikTok’s data never leaves TikTok-controlled systems. Whether buying sponsored placements or extending programmatic campaigns, all targeting decisions are made within infrastructure that TikTok owns and controls — Genuin provides the inventory surface, not the targeting logic.
5.1  Sponsorships — Bring Your Own Algorithm
For Sponsored Takeover and Sponsored Post, TikTok can connect its own targeting algorithm directly to the Genuin feed. Rather than delegating audience selection to Genuin, TikTok’s server makes the targeting decision for every impression in real time.
5.2  Programmatic Audience Extension — Programmatic Controlled Targeting
For Programmatic Audience Extension, targeting is owned end-to-end by TikTok’s demand-side platform. Genuin’s role is to surface the impression opportunity and pass standard bid request signals through the programmatic pipe — audience matching, frequency controls, and creative selection are all decisions that happen within TikTok’s programmatic interface, not on Genuin’s infrastructure.
